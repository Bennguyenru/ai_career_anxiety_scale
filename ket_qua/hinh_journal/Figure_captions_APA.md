*Measurement Model of AI Career Anxiety and Related Constructs*

**Figure 3**

*Discriminant Validity Among AI Career Anxiety, Threat Appraisal, AI Self-Efficacy, and Career Self-Efficacy*

*Note.* *N* = 464. Panel (a): heterotrait-monotrait ratios (Henseler et al., 2015); the outlined cell is at or above .85. Panel (b): latent correlations with 95% confidence intervals; the shaded band marks the .85 to 1.0 range. ANX and THR correlate *r* = .87, 95% CI [.80, .94]; the interval excludes 1.0, and a two-factor model fit better than a single merged factor, Satorra-Bentler Δχ²(1) = 21.57, *p* < .001.

**Figure 4**

*Measurement Invariance of the AI Career Anxiety Scale Across Discipline, Year of Study, and Gender*

*Note.* Multigroup confirmatory factor analysis of the nine-item one-factor model with MLR. Discipline = information technology versus other disciplines; Year = Years 1 and 2 versus Year 3 and above; Gender = male versus female (respondents who reported another gender or preferred not to say were excluded from this comparison). Shaded areas mark good fit. Panel (c) shows the drop in robust CFI when loadings (metric) and then intercepts (scalar) were constrained equal; values at or below .010 support invariance (Chen, 2007), and negative values indicate improved fit.
